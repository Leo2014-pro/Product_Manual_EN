# Onboarding

### Merchant Onboarding (API Integration)

**Process:**
- Submit business information
- Risk review
- Account activation
- Start trading

### SaaS Partner

**Onboarding Process:**
- Submit partnership application — contact us at contact@temar.ai with basic business information
- Requirements discussion and solution confirmation — align on business model, payment structure, and fund model (custodial / non-custodial)
- Platform activation and configuration — set up SaaS console, account system, and related capability modules
- Integration testing and go-live — complete system testing, then enter production and officially launch business

**Suitable for payment platforms:**
- Independent backend
- Multi-merchant system
- Channel management capabilities
- Fund system capabilities
