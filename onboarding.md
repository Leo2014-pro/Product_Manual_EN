# Onboarding


### Merchant Onboarding (API Integration)

**Process:**
- Submit company information
- Risk review
- Account activation
- Begin transacting

### SaaS Partner

**Onboarding Process:**
- Submit partnership application — Contact us at contact@temar.ai and provide basic business information
- Requirement discussion and solution confirmation — Align on business model, payment structure, and fund model (custodial / non-custodial)
- Platform provisioning and configuration — Activate SaaS console, account system, and related capability modules
- Integration testing and go-live — Complete system testing before entering production and officially launching business

**Suitable for payment platforms:**
- Independent backend
- Multi-merchant system
- Channel management capabilities
- Fund infrastructure capabilities
