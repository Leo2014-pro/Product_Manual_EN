# Platform Definition

This platform is a **Global Payment Infrastructure Platform** designed to provide enterprises with the foundational capabilities needed to build payment systems.

The platform does not serve directly as a fund management tool; rather, it provides a complete payment infrastructure layer, including:

- Global collection network access capabilities
- Card payment and acquiring capabilities
- Card issuing capabilities
- Virtual accounts and ledger system
- Fund settlement and exchange capabilities
- Multi-channel routing and scheduling capabilities

Enterprises can leverage this platform to rapidly build their own payment products and fund flow systems.
