# Card Acquiring

## Card Collection Orders

**Feature Description**: View merchant credit card collection orders, supporting queries on order status, payment information, fees, and order details.

---

## Refund Orders

**Feature Description**: View merchant credit card refund orders, supporting queries on refund status, refund amount, original payment order, and refund processing records.

---

## Dispute Orders

**Feature Description**: View merchant credit card dispute (chargeback) orders, supporting queries on dispute status, processing progress, evidence materials, and final resolution results.
